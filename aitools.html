How each AI tool talks to Plexiform and to the other AI sessions running here. Fix adds Plexiform's entry to that tool's own settings and changes nothing else.

    Add a custom tool

    For anything without hooks of its own (Aider, OpenCode, Copilot CLI, Amp…). Run it as plexiform-run <command> and it appears in the widget as working, then done or failed. That is all it can report: it cannot answer prompts or show what the tool is doing.

    Your team boards can also be offered inside AI apps: that is in Settings → Advanced.

    Connecting edits only the tool's own hook settings, never its chats or logins. A copy of the file is kept next to it first (.plexiform-backup-…) and Undo puts it back. Plexiform reads no conversation text; it sees that a session started, worked, or finished.
