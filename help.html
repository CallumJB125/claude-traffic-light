    The little chips

      “Needs your input” vs “Waiting for you”

      Needs your input (amber, waving): An AI agent has paused mid-task on a permission prompt or a question. Nothing moves until you answer in the terminal.

      Waiting for you: An AI agent finished its turn. It's simply your turn to type — nothing is stuck.

      What the colours usually mean

      Green: working, or just finished. Amber: needs you, a failed turn, or nothing running. Red: out of tokens, offline, a runaway session, or over budget. Every colour is programmable in Widget configuration, so yours may differ.

      Spend: budgets and runaway sessions

      Buddy prices every turn from your Claude Code transcripts at API list prices. Set a daily or weekly budget in Preferences → Spend: near it (80% by default) the light goes amber with money eyes when a turn ends; over it, red with money eyes.

      Runaway session (pulsing red, wide eyes; hover the widget for the burn rate, like “$47.20 in 18 min”): one session spent more than your threshold ($40 in 20 minutes by default) and is still working. You get one notification; click it to jump to that terminal. Buddy never types into or stops a session you started.

      On a Pro or Max plan you aren't billed per token, so these are API-price equivalents, not a share of your usage window.

      Ask him out loud

      Press and hold the widget (or hold the key you picked in Preferences → Voice), ask, and let go. He answers “What's blocked?”, “What are my sessions doing?”, “How much have I spent today?” and “What happened while I was out?”. The red mic badge shows whenever he's listening; speech becomes text on this Mac and audio is never kept. A teammate’s AI activity needs the team board.

      Reopen from the ? on the widget, or open Help in the main app.
