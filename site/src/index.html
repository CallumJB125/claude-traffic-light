What stays on your Mac is almost everything.

No telemetry. No analytics. No account needed to use the widget. Your prompts and Claude's replies are never stored by {{brand.name}}, and the conversation logs it reads to count tokens and cost never leave your computer.

The one time data leaves is when you join a team board: your team's hub then receives your cards, handovers and run progress, with credential-like text and home-folder paths filtered out first. Sharing what you're working on with teammates is a separate opt-in.

Read the privacy notice

  • Voice questions are turned into text on your device and are off by default.
  • Calendar access is off by default, and event titles aren't read unless you turn that on.
  • Crash reports, when they arrive, will be opt-in.
  • Your rules, settings and cameo photos stay in a folder on your Mac.

Questions people ask first

Which agents does it work with?

Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and Gemini report their state through hooks, and {{brand.name}} turns each into the same lamps. Claude Code has the deepest support today.

Does it read my prompts or my code?

It never stores prompts. It reads Claude Code's conversation logs on your Mac only to count tokens and cost, and keeps the numbers. Code stays in your repo.

What does it send to you?

Nothing. There's no telemetry or analytics. If you join a team board, your team's hub receives what the board needs, described in the privacy notice.

Why do I have to right-click to open it?

The beta isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS asks you to confirm the first time. The steps are on the download page.

Is there a Windows or Linux version?

Builds are being prepared. The download page lists whatever is published right now.

Who is Buddy?

The pixel character. {{brand.name}} was called Claude Buddy while it was being built; the character kept the name.
