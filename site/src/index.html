Almost everything stays on your computer.

The app sends no telemetry and no analytics, and the widget needs no account. Your prompts and Claude's replies are never stored by {{brand.name}}, and the conversation logs it reads to count tokens and cost never leave your computer.

Data leaves when you join a team board: your team's hub then receives your cards, handovers and run progress, with credential-like text and home-folder paths filtered out first, and signing in to a hub shares your account name and email with it. Sharing what you're working on with teammates is a separate opt-in.

This summary follows the beta's privacy notice, which is a draft pending legal review. Hub hosting, retention and deletion terms are being written.

Questions people ask first

Which agents does it work with?

Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and Gemini report their state through hooks, and {{brand.name}} turns each into the same lamps. Claude Code has the deepest support today.

Does it read my prompts or my code?

It never stores prompts. It reads Claude Code's conversation logs on your Mac only to count tokens and cost, and keeps the numbers. Code stays in your repo.

What does the app send out?

No telemetry and no analytics. If you join a team board, your team's hub receives what the board needs and your account details, described in the privacy notice.

Why does the first open take extra steps?

The beta isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS asks you to allow it once in System Settings. The steps are on the download page.

Is there a Windows or Linux version?

Builds are being prepared. The download page lists whatever is published right now.

Who is Buddy?

The pixel character. {{brand.name}} was called Claude Buddy while it was being built; the character kept the name.

How do I get in?

You can install the desktop app now. Joining a team board needs an invite: join the waitlist below, and beta spots go to small teams first.
