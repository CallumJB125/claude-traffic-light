Icon directions

Mycelium and mushrooms: a mushroom whose roots are a network. Each is shown large, at the sizes it really appears (dock 64, taskbar 32, tab and tray 16), on light and dark. Pick by looking; the other two go away.

A. Lamp cap

Flat and quiet. The three dots are the three lamps, so the mushroom is a traffic light. Reads at 16 px as a cap and stem.
